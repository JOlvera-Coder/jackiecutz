Terms & Conditions

1. Appointments & Punctuality

Clients are encouraged to arrive 5 to 10 minutes prior to scheduled appointments. A strict 15-minute grace period applies. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may be shortened to accommodate subsequent clients or subject to rescheduling.

2. 24-Hour Cancellation & No-Show Policy

We require a minimum of 24 hours advance notice to cancel or reschedule any appointment. If you need to cancel or change your time, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance by calling or texting (832) 353-4577 or through your client account. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or missed appointments without notice (No-Shows) may require a non-refundable deposit for future bookings.

3. Services & Payments

All prices for haircutting, styling, beard trims, chemical treatments, and retail products are final. Payment in full is due immediately upon completion of services via approved studio payment methods.

4. Client Safety & Studio Etiquette

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone demonstrating disrespectful behavior, intoxication, or showing signs of contagious scalp/skin conditions to ensure health and hygiene standards.

5. Privacy & Information Use

Customer contact information (name, phone number, email address, and birthday) is collected solely for appointment reminders, profile management, and studio updates. Information is strictly confidential and never sold to third parties.
