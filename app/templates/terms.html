1. Appointment & Cancellation Policy

Appointments are held with a 10-minute grace window. If you arrive later than 10 minutes past your reserved time, your slot may be forfeited to accommodate walk-ins. We require at least 2 hours advance notice for cancellations or rescheduling.

2. Card Holds & Service Payment

When booking online, card details may be securely tokenized via 256-bit encryption as a reservation hold. Your card will not be charged until your service is completed in the salon chair. You may settle your final balance via Cash, Zelle, Cash App, or In-Salon Card Tap.

3. Privacy Policy

Jackiecutz Hair Studio respects your privacy. We only collect essential booking contact details (name, phone number, style preferences) solely for appointment confirmations, reminders, and customer profile management. Your information is never sold or shared with third parties.
