Studio Policies, Terms & Privacy

Jackiecutz Hair Studio • Divine Salon Suite 105

1. Right to Refuse Service

Jackiecutz Hair Studio reserves the right to refuse service to anyone demonstrating disruptive behavior, intoxication, non-compliance with health or safety protocols, or requests that compromise scalp or hair integrity.

2. Privacy, Communications & Marketing Consent

By creating an account, you expressly consent to receive salon communications from Jackiecutz Hair Studio, including:

  • Appointment confirmations, waitlist reminders, and schedule updates via SMS and Phone.
  • Exclusive promotional offers, seasonal discounts, and birthday specials delivered via Email, SMS, and in-app notifications.
  • Industry-standard record keeping of styling preferences and service history to ensure tailored hair care.

We value your privacy. Your personal information will never be sold, rented, or distributed to third-party advertisers.

3. Appointments, Grace Window & Cancellations

We provide a 10-minute grace window for all scheduled bookings. Arrivals beyond 10 minutes may need to be rescheduled or transitioned to walk-in status to protect other client reservations. We respectfully request at least 2 hours advance notice for any cancellation or schedule change.

4. Chemical Services & Health Disclosure

Clients receiving color, bleach, or intensive treatments must disclose any known skin allergies, sensitivities, or previous home hair coloring history. Strand tests and patch consultations may be required prior to service.

5. Payments & Gratuities

Payments are collected upon completion of service. Cash is preferred; Zelle, Cash App, and major Debit/Credit cards are welcomed.
